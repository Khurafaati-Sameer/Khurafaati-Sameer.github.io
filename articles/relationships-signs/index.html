20 Signs Someone
May Be Interested
in You

कुछ इशारे शब्दों में नहीं,
ख़ामोशियों में कहे जाते हैं।
Writer: Khurafaati Sameer • quiet-observations-01

कुछ बातें लिखी नहीं जातीं।
महसूस हो जाती हैं।

Har kisi ne ye lamha jiya hai. Saamne wala baat to karta hai, par aap sochte rehte ho — kya ye sirf dosti hai, ya isse zyada?

Psychology kehta hai, pyaar ya interest ek shabd se nahi banta. Wo ek pattern se banta hai. Ek din ka pyaar bhara message kuch nahi kehta, lekin roz ki choti-choti fikar bahut kuch keh jaati hai.

Main kahaniyan nahi likhta. Main un khamoshiyon ko likhta hoon jinhe log aksar samajh nahi paate. Ye 20 nishaniyan unhi khamoshiyon ki dictionary hain.

01 — CHAPTER I: WORDS / शब्द
01

Wo pehle baat shuru karte hain.

Interest hamesha effort maangta hai. Agar har baar conversation ki shuruaat wahi se ho rahi hai, to samjho aap unke din ka hissa ho.

02

Wo choti details yaad rakhte hain.

Aapne 3 hafte pehle bola tha ki aapko tez chai pasand hai. Aur unhe abhi tak yaad hai. Ye memory nahi, attention hai.

03

Wo dhyan se sunte hain.

Phone side me, aankhon me dekh kar. Beech me kaat-te nahi. Jaise aapki baat hi sabse important ho.

04

Follow-up karte hain.

Milne ke baad “ghar pahunch gaye?” ya “aaj baat karke accha laga.” Ye ek chota sa sentence batata hai ki aap unke zehen me reh gaye ho.

05

Aapki jeet ko apni jeet samajhte hain.

Aapki choti si khushi par bhi aankhon me chamak. Bina jealousy, bina comparison.

02 — CHAPTER II: SILENCE / ख़ामोशी
06

Lambi eye contact.

Nazrein churane ke bajay thodi der tak dekhna aur halki si muskurahat. Ye aankhon ki zubaan hai.

07

Open body language.

Aapse baat karte hue aapki taraf jhukna, haath na baandhna. Jism jhooth nahi bolta.

08

Aapke aas-paas thoda nervous hona.

Awaaz ka thoda tez ho jana, haathon ka hilna. Ye bachkani ghabrahat hi sabse sachcha signal hai.

09

Aapko hasane ki koshish.

Halki teasing, beech ke andar ke jokes. Taaki aap dono ki ek alag duniya ban jaye.

10

Mirroring.

Aap jaise baithe ho, waise hi wo. Psychology me ise rapport kehte hain — jab do log ek hi rhythm me aa jate hain.

03 — CHAPTER III: EFFORT / कोशिश
11

Aapke liye waqt nikalte hain.

Sab busy hain. Lekin jo interested hota hai, wo waqt nikalta hai, bahana nahi.

12

Chote-chote efforts.

Aapka favourite gana bhejna, aapke kaam me bina bole help kar dena. Pyaar bade tohfon me nahi, in choton me rehta hai.

13

Aapki duniya me curiosity.

Sirf aapke looks me nahi, aapke sapnon, aapke dar, aapke ghar walon me interest lena.

14

Aapke logon ko izzat dena.

Aapke doston ke baare me puchna, kyunki wo jaante hain — jo aapke liye important hai, wo unke liye bhi important hona chahiye.

15

Future plans me aapko shamil karna.

“Kabhi saath Gangotri chalenge” ya “Wo kitaab saath padhenge.” Ye future ka ek chota sa invitation hai.

16

Aapke mood ko bina bole samajh jana.

Aap thoda khamosh ho, aur wo puch le — “sab theek hai na?” Ye empathy hai.

17

Aapko priority dena.

Bheed me bhi aapki baat ko pehle sunna.

04 — CHAPTER IV: RESPECT / इज्ज़त
18

Aapki boundaries ka respect.

Ye sabse bada green flag hai. Na ka matlab na samajhna. Aapko kabhi uncomfortable mehsoos na karwana.

19

Wo batate hain ki wo available hain.

Directly ya indirectly ye batana ki wo kisi aur ko nahi dekh rahe.

20

Consistency.

Ek din bahut pyaar aur 5 din gayab — ye interest nahi, confusion hai. Jo sach me interested hai, wo consistent rehta hai. Thoda, lekin roz.

Ant me ek sach

Ek friendly insaan bhi ye sab kar sakta hai. Isliye ek rule yaad rakho:

Pattern dekho, pal nahi. Ek din ka pyaar kuch nahi kehta, roz ki choti fikar sab kuch kehti hai.

Aur agar ye rishta aapke liye important hai, to andaze lagane se behtar hai — pooch lena. Seedha keh dena — “Mujhe tumhare saath waqt bitana accha lagta hai. Kya hum kabhi chai pe mil sakte hain?”

Aur agar jawab ‘na’ hai, to us ‘na’ ko bhi utni hi izzat se sunna.

Kyunki body language ishara de sakti hai, lekin sukoon hamesha saaf baat se hi aata hai.

quiet observations • for the attentive reader

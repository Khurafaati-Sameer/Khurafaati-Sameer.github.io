20 Signs Someone May Be Interested in You

Kisi ke behaviour ko samajhne ke liye common social aur psychological signals dekhiye, bina har signal ko proof samjhe.

By Khurafaati Sameer • 18 August 2026 • 5 min read
#Relationships   #Psychology   #Communication

Kisi ke behaviour se interest ka andaaza lagaya ja sakta hai, lekin koi single sign feelings ka final proof nahi hota.

Common signals

Conversation start karna, details yaad rakhna, time nikalna, genuine curiosity dikhana aur consistent communication kuch common indicators ho sakte hain.

Lekin context zaroori hai. Friendly behaviour ko romantic interest samajhna aasaan hai, isliye ek pattern ko waqt ke saath dekhna better hota hai.

Best approach

Agar relationship important hai, guessing games ke bajay respectful aur clear communication sabse healthy option hai. Boundaries aur consent ko hamesha priority dein.

Bottom line

Body language aur behaviour clues de sakte hain; certainty communication se aati hai.
